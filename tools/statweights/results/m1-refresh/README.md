# M1 refreshed stat weights

Runs against WoWSims Forever commit `16e85426c43340f73f4f6ddc84938d4385890ea8` (MIT, copyright 2022 wowsims team), 2026-11-07. The raw protojson outputs are beside this report. Values are unverified in game. Inputs are the files at that sim commit, except the recorded Bear build. The wrapper uses `core.StatWeights`, seed 101, target EP interval 0.05 at 90%, and maximum iteration multiplier 3000. Check sample counts and intervals in the raw results: the target is subject to the cap. The `+-` printed by the wrapper is a standard deviation, not the confidence interval.

- Affliction: `ui/specs/warlock/dps/builds/sm_ruin.build.json`, `ui/specs/warlock/dps/gear_sets/preraid.gear.json`; spell damage reference. `warlock-new.json`.
- Enhancement: `ui/specs/shaman/enhancement/builds/testing.build.json`; attack power reference, hit rating offset -40, main-hand DPS. `shaman-new.json`.
- Bear: `bear-new.build.json`, derived from the previous M1 Bear build with strength of earth, grace of air and might changed from booleans to rank 1, and four renamed raid buffs mapped to their current names. Agility reference; the same level 63, 6000 base damage encounter and healing model as the original M1 run. `bear-new.json`.
- Protection: `ui/specs/paladin/protection/defaults.siml`; target base damage overridden to 6000, strength reference, main-hand DPS. `prot-new.json`.
- Retribution: `ui/specs/paladin/retribution/defaults.siml`, with `ui/specs/paladin/retribution/apls/default.apl.siml` replacing its simple rotation. Attack power reference, main-hand DPS. `ret-new.json`; MP5 was measured separately against attack power with the same inputs in `ret-mp5.json`. Hit uses the separate `ret-hit-minus40.json` run with 40 hit rating removed: the unadjusted hit EP is 0.6411, and with the offset it is 6.8769. This below-cap value avoids rating hit near the cap for every player.

DPS tables use signed raw DPS weights divided by the raw reference weight. For each tank stat, the table uses 0.6 times the raw TPS weight divided by the TPS strength weight plus 1.15 times the raw TMI weight divided by the TMI stamina weight, as in the prior M1 tank report. These are group-tank weights. Generic spell power is the sum of the spell-damage and healing-power contributions; in these runs the healing contribution is zero. Armor penetration and other stats without canonical item keys are omitted. Each computed table is per rating point.

Restoration Druid is team judgement, not computed: healing power and generic spell power 1; intellect 0.3, spirit 0.2, MP5 0.5, crit rating 0.2, haste rating 0.5 and stamina 0.1. These are deliberately approximate priorities for testing healing loadouts, not a prediction from a healing simulation. The sim identifies Restoration Druid as a gear planner with no implemented healing spells. The table is unverified in game.

The Enhancement and Retribution tables use below-cap hit values and can overvalue hit near a cap. The tank tables use placeholder gear and a specific encounter. None adapts to the player's talents, encounter or caps.

## Preset cross-check

The sim's current Unsealed Wrath preset has hit 6.83 and haste 22.87, versus 6.8769 with the hit offset and 3.1594 haste in these runs. The hit-offset experiment explains the hit difference from the unadjusted run. The preset's haste 22.87 dates to sim commit `1258b9781a` and the old Twist of Light build. Commit `db28d84a8a` renamed that preset to Unsealed Wrath without recalculating its weights while replacing the talent and rebuilding the tree. This run measures the new talent and current rotation, so the old snapshot is not a measurement of this build. The separate contribution of each change to the haste difference is unverified. The Enhancement preset has hit 5.2 and spell damage 1.27 versus 8.4048 and 2.2487 here; the preset numbers date to `07fec950bb` and were measured on an older sim. The earlier same-input reproduction and later differences are documented in `docs/sim-learnings.md` section 4; the individual mechanics responsible for the gap remain unverified. Bear's preset uses a different tank combination (including DTPS), so its values are not directly comparable to the TPS/TMI recipe. Protection and Affliction have no comparable preset in the documented source list. These differences do not verify the tables in game.
